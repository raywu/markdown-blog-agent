# Editorial corrections

These examples show how to infer an editorial rule. They are not writing templates.

## Remove explanation already implied

Agent:
> Despite the title, it isn't tied to ChatGPT. The repo is agent agnostic.

Author:
> Remove "Despite the title, it isn't tied to ChatGPT."

Learn:
The second sentence already communicates the relevant information. Delete the throat-clearing sentence.

Do not learn:
Always remove sentences beginning with "Despite the title."

## Reduce grandiosity globally

Author:
> Make it even less grandiose and approachable. Heavily remove any declaration or pronouncement. Shorten every sentence. Remove adjectives or adverbs.

Learn:
Audit the whole draft for declarations, broad claims, emphasis, modifiers, and long explanatory sentences. Apply the correction globally.

Do not learn:
Every sentence must be a fragment or contain no modifiers.

## Preserve author wording

Author:
> But the path is fixed. If I understand an idea but keep missing one concept, it can't change the lesson around that.

Learn:
When the author's wording is clear and fits the intended voice, preserve it rather than offering a more polished paraphrase.

## Reduce personal setup

Author:
> There's too much personal explanation. Reduce them.

Learn:
Shorten background that delays the post's actual subject. Check the rest of the opening for the same problem.

## Short CTA

Author:
> Give it a try with your agent. Open an issue or submit a PR—contributions are welcome!

Learn:
A call to action can be direct and short.

Do not learn:
Every post should end with this sentence.
